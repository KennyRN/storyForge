# INTEGRATION-PROMPT — merge corpus-webnovel v1.1.0 into `western-serial`

You are integrating three corpus-derived patterns into the **existing**
`western-serial` generator (`src/titleforge/lexicons/westernSerial.ts`). This is
**additive and reversible**: you add 6 patterns, 9 lexicon slots, and 1 platform
option. You change nothing that is already there. If anything below conflicts
with the live code, **trust the code** and stop — the source of truth is the
repo, not this document.

## The change

Corpus v1.1.0 was built from 311 anchor-sourced titles across two metric axes
(103 Goodreads-rated by ratings; 50 Royal Road by followers) plus 148 frame-coverage
(RR tracker, romance apps, and the Aethon/Shadow Alley publisher catalogues),
Dreame/GoodNovel/NovelCat/AlphaNovel),
filtered to the above-median-ratings pool (median 9,463; top quartile 17,030).
Weights and the promoted family set are derived from that selected pool.

Apply `PATCH.txt` (in this folder), generated by `derive.mjs`:

1. Append the 6 pattern objects to `westernSerialLexicon.patterns`:
   `dungeon-anchor`, `regression-rebirth`, `possessive-relation`, `interrogative-hook`, `status-hook`, `bracket-tag`.
2. Merge the 9 slots into `westernSerialLexicon.lexicon`:
   `monster`, `rebirth`, `rebirthTail`, `interro`, `owner`, `relObject`, `statusVerb`, `beloved`, `bracketTag`.
3. Add `{ "id": "webnovel", "label": "WebNovel" }` to
   `westernSerialLexicon.platforms`.
4. **Engine change (one file):** add the acronym dictionary to
   `engine/titlecase.ts` (an `ACRONYMS` map + `applyAcronym` hook in
   `titleCase`) so genre acronyms survive casing — `LitRPG` not `Litrpg`,
   `CEO's` not `Ceo's`. This is additive: it only alters words whose letter
   core is a known acronym; all other output is unchanged. It affects every
   generator, which is the intent. A pre-applied `westernSerial.applied.ts` and
   the patched `titlecase.ts` are included, so you can diff rather than merge.

Do not reorder, reweight, or reword existing patterns. Do not touch any other
lexicon, the `engine/`, or the `series`/`novel` lanes.

## Invariants (each traceable to a real mechanism or a failure seen while building)

1. **Nine generator ids, unchanged.** `__tests__/lexicons.structural.test.ts`
   asserts the exact set of nine ids. This work extends `western-serial` in
   place; it must NOT add a tenth generator. (Mechanism: the `toEqual([...])`
   assertion on `ALL_TITLEFORGE_LEXICONS`.)

2. **`validateSpec` and `checkArticleAgreement` return `[]`.** Verified by
   `verify.mjs` against the live engine. In particular every literal `the` in a
   new template precedes a **bare** slot (`role`, `rank`) — never an articled
   one. (Failure seen: an early draft that referenced platform `webnovel`
   without declaring it failed `validateSpec`; fixed by adding the platform
   option — hence step 3 is not optional.)

3. **No unindexed repeated slot.** `validateTemplate` fails a template that uses
   the same slot twice without `#1`/`#2`. None of the new templates repeat a
   slot; keep it that way if you edit them.

4. **The a/an trap.** `rebirth` openers deliberately end in an article
   (`Reincarnated as the`, `I Became the`) so `{rebirth} {role}` reads cleanly
   and `{rebirth} {rank} {role}` never produces "as a F-Rank". The engine has no
   a/an fixer; do not add rebirth openers ending in a bare "a".

5. **Tag comprehensively or not at all.** The 4 new slots are intentionally
   **untagged** (genre-neutral), so they render under every genre their patterns
   declare. `withTags` is forgiving only when a slot has *no* matching entry; if
   you later tag *some* entries in these slots, tag *all* of them or the untagged
   ones become unreachable under that genre. (Mechanism: `withTags` in
   `engine/lexicon.ts`.)

6. **Every pattern keeps a non-empty `note` and `exemplar`.** The structural
   test enforces this. Every new exemplar is a **verified English-original**
   corpus title (`Cultivating Dungeon`, `Reincarnation: I Am the Villainess and
   Love Target?!`, `Carl's Doomsday Scenario`, `Awakened As A Dungeon Core?`); `verify.mjs` re-checks each
   against `corpus.jsonl`. Do not swap in an exemplar that isn't in the corpus,
   and never invent one.

7. **Scope firewall.** This lane is anglophone / English-original web fiction.
   Translated JP/KR/CN/SEA titles are `scope=reference-translated` in the corpus
   and are excluded from derivation. Do not promote them into exemplars or word
   lists — those traditions have their own generators.

8. **Weights stay commensurate and honest.** New weights (5/3/2) sit inside the
   shipped 2–4 range plus one dominant 5. `dungeon-anchor` is deliberately
   **capped** below its raw corpus share because that share is an over-sampling
   artefact (recorded in `frozen/webnovel.v1.0.0.json` and the provenance). If
   you regrow the corpus, re-run the pipeline rather than hand-editing weights.

10. **Acronym casing is engine-level.** The `ACRONYMS` map lives in
   `titlecase.ts`, not the lexicon, so any generator benefits and the list is
   maintained in one place. Extend the map, don't pre-case glosses — the caser
   re-derives casing from scratch and would flatten a hand-cased gloss.

9. **Bundle cost.** 6 patterns + 9 short slots ≈ a small, bounded addition to
   the second-largest lexicon. The corpus-webnovel/ tooling is dev-time only
   (`.mjs`, not imported by the plugin entry) and must never be bundled.
